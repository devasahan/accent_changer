      Long check: what the meeting hears

      Records CABLE Output, the microphone the meeting uses. With the test tone the meeting hears a steady tone instead of your voice until the check ends.

      Hide the app

      The app disappears from the screen and the taskbar and comes back by itself after 30 minutes, without taking over your keyboard (look for it on the taskbar). To bring it back sooner, open Accent Changer from the Start menu. If the long check is running, it waits until 2 minutes after the app is back.
